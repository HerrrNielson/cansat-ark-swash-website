Support us

Help us build a satellite.

Team SWASH is a student project at HTL Kaindorf. We are looking for companies and people to support us with circuit boards, materials and tests.

What for

What we need support for.

01

Circuit boards

Our electronics consist of our own circuit boards, which we have manufactured and assembled. For prototypes we need more than one set.

02

Rotor and structure

We build rotor blades, hub, bearings, servos and the can itself several times until everything holds together and works reliably.

03

Tests

Before launch we test the rotor in drop tests. For this we need spare parts and test materials.

How

How you can help.

  • With a sponsorship or a donation.
  • With components, materials or manufacturing, for example circuit boards or 3D printing.
  • With know-how in electronics, aerodynamics or manufacturing.
  • With contacts to companies, schools or the media.

What you get from us

We name our supporters on this website and on Instagram, with their logo if agreed. And we keep you up to date on our progress until launch.
