Team SWASH

Four disciplines, one CanSat.

We are a six-member team from the 2023 cohort at HTL Kaindorf, developing our system together — from mechanics and electronics to flight software and scientific analysis.

01

Engineering

Mechanics & CAD

We design the rotor, housing and components, and check how everything fits within the CanSat requirements.

02

Electronics

Circuit board & sensors

We integrate the flight computer, sensors, GPS, radio and power supply into a compact system.

03

Software

Flight control & ground station

We develop flight phases, telemetry, the ground-station link and the camera software.

04

Science

Mission & analysis

We plan measurements, analyse NDVI image data and document experiments and results.
