Instructions

How the simulation works

  1. Start: Click “OK” in the “Launch day simulation” window. Default: random faults, launch after 1 minute in real time; you can change the time on the pad and the speed there.
  2. Watch: Flight phase, sensors and telemetry are on the left. In the middle, “Mission” shows the CanSat in 3D, the instruments and the flight path, next to charts, map and received packets. Important events are called out by voice.
  3. React: Red dots in the checklist on the right show what to do. Send commands under “Commands”, for example “Pi active” right after the simulation starts, or “FORCE ROTOR DEPLOY” if the rotor does not open.
  4. Instructor: In the “Launch day simulation” panel, launch the rocket with “Launch now”. “Reveal” shows the hidden faults.
