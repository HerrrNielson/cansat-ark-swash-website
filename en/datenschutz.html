Privacy

Privacy policy

We collect as little data as possible. This page explains what data is still processed and what rights you have.

At a glance

  • The website sets no cookies. For visitor statistics we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, also without cookies.
  • When you visit the website, the hosting provider processes technically necessary connection data such as your IP address.
  • If you change the colour scheme, your browser remembers it. This setting stays on your device.
  • If you write to us, we use your details only to reply.

2. Visiting the website

When you visit this website, our hosting provider automatically processes data sent by your browser: IP address, date and time, page requested, amount of data transferred, browser and operating system, and the previously visited page if your browser sends it. This is necessary to deliver the website, keep it running reliably and fend off attacks.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not use this data to analyse visitors.

The hosting provider is Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. Cloudflare automatically deletes access logs in our Cloudflare account after 7 days at most. How long Cloudflare keeps connection data to protect its own network is governed by Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Visitor statistics

To see how often our pages are viewed, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics. For this, each page loads a small script from Cloudflare (static.cloudflareinsights.com). When a page is viewed, it sends the page address, the previously visited page, browser, operating system, device type, the country derived from the IP address and loading times to Cloudflare. Cloudflare sets no cookies for this, stores nothing on your device and, according to Cloudflare, does not create profiles of visitors. We only see aggregated figures, for example how often a page was viewed.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in improving the website and evaluating our outreach for the competition (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time, for example by blocking scripts from static.cloudflareinsights.com in your browser.

3. Settings in your browser

The website itself sets no cookies. If you change the colour scheme (light or dark) or acknowledge the privacy notice, the website saves this choice in your browser's local storage so that it is kept for your next visit. On the Simulation page, your browser also remembers that you have seen the instructions and which items you have ticked in the simulation's checklist. These entries contain only this information, are not sent to us and can be deleted at any time in your browser settings.

This storage is strictly necessary to provide the setting you requested (Section 165(3) of the Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021).

4. Contacting us by email

If you email us, we process your email address, your name if provided, and the content of your message in order to answer your enquiry. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). If your enquiry concerns a collaboration such as a sponsorship, the legal basis is the preparation of that agreement (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

The addresses on the contact page run through the HTL Kaindorf email system, the address above through Apple iCloud. We delete your message once your enquiry has been dealt with, at the latest when the project ends, unless we are legally required to keep it longer.

5. Contact form

When you submit the form on the contact page, your browser sends your name, email address, optionally your phone number and your message to the form service Web3Forms (web3forms.com). Web3Forms forwards the message to our team by email. We use this information only to answer your enquiry (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and delete it once the enquiry has been dealt with. Data may be processed outside the EU in the process. If you do not want this, please email us directly instead.

6. Team photos and names

We publish the names, photos and responsibilities of our team members only with their consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). Consent can be withdrawn at any time; we will then remove the details from the website.

7. Links to other websites

This website links to other websites, for example ESERO Austria or our Instagram profile. We do not embed any Instagram content. Data is only transmitted to the respective operator once you click such a link. Their privacy policy then applies.

8. Recipients

Your data is only received by those who need it for the purposes stated: the hosting provider Cloudflare (also for visitor statistics), the form service Web3Forms for messages sent via the contact form and, for emails, the operator of the respective email system, i.e. HTL Kaindorf or Apple. We do not sell data or pass it on for advertising.

Cloudflare is a US company certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Data may therefore be transferred to the USA on the basis of the European Commission's adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). Apple bases transfers from the EU to the USA on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).

9. Your rights

You have the right to access the data stored about you (Article 15 GDPR), and to rectification (Article 16), erasure (Article 17), restriction of processing (Article 18) and data portability (Article 20). You may object to processing based on legitimate interests (Article 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future.

To do so, simply write to the address given above. If you believe we are not processing your data correctly, you can complain to the Austrian Data Protection Authority: Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb.gv.at.

10. Other information

You do not have to give us any data to use this website, although we cannot reply without an email address. We do not make automated decisions or create profiles (Article 22 GDPR).

We update this policy when the website or the law changes. The version published here applies. Last updated: November 2026.
