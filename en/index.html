Team SWASH · CanSat Austria

We are bringing a satellite the size of a can safely back to Earth.

Our CanSat measures temperature and air pressure during descent, sends the data to a ground station and tests controlled autorotation for a precise landing.

Our motivation

Why we take part

We are taking part in CanSat Austria because we want to design, build and test technology ourselves, not only discuss it in class. The project brings mechanics, electronics, programming and scientific analysis together. We are especially interested in transmitting reliable measurements and developing a controlled descent. The competition lets us test our ideas and document the results clearly.

Our CanSat

Mission in detail

What we carry on board.

We combine instruments, flight control and image analysis in one compact system.

01

Measurement data

Temperature and air pressure are recorded during descent and transmitted to the ground station via LoRa.

02

Autorotation

A rotor and active control are intended to stabilise the descent and enable a precise landing.

03

Camera & NDVI

NDVI is a vegetation index that uses image data to indicate plant health. A camera is intended to capture the images needed for this analysis.

From testing to flight

We share what works — and what we are still learning.

Our results page collects preliminary tests and will add measurements and analysis after the flight.

We are taking part in CanSat Austria

CanSat Austria is a school competition organised by ESERO Austria. Teams build a satellite the size of a drinks can and carry out their own mission. Team SWASH is taking part in the 2026/27 season.
